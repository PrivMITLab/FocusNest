Privacy Philosophy

Digital wellbeing should never require handing your behavioral history to a cloud server. FocusNest omits the Android INTERNET permission entirely. Everything stays on your device in Room SQLite and DataStore.

Technical Architecture

Jetpack Compose & Material 3

Adaptive Compact/Medium/Expanded layouts with custom Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans & JetBrains Mono typography and 5 accent palettes.

Room + DataStore + WorkManager

18 indexed Room entities, epoch-anchored reboot-safe timer persistence, and 15-minute background usage sync.
