Privacy Philosophy

Digital wellbeing should never require handing your behavioral history to a cloud server. FocusNest omits the Android INTERNET permission entirely. Everything stays on your device in Room SQLite and DataStore.

Technical Architecture

Jetpack Compose & Material 3

Adaptive Compact/Medium/Expanded layouts with custom Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans & JetBrains Mono typography, English/Hindi localization, and 6 accent palettes.

Room v2 + DataStore + WorkManager

21 indexed Room entities with non-destructive migrations, monotonic elapsedRealtime timer protection, and 15-minute background usage sync.

Android 8–16 Compatibility

Play-compliant launcher queries, ForegroundService specialUse declarations, BiometricPrompt, and Realme/OEM optimization guidance.
